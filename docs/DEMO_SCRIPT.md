# Financial planning demo (90 seconds)

**0:00–0:25 — Budget to next funding date.** Show a hypothetical student with £1,000 accessible bank balance and £20 cash, £300 confirmed future funding, £700 future essentials/required payments, £200 emergency reserve and £300 whole-period spending allowance. Explain the £120 estimated headroom and £10/day allowance. Prior transactions are already included in today's balance.

**0:25–0:45 — Avoid false confidence.** Raise the allowance to £500. The plan displays an £80 shortfall. An edited budget requires reconfirmation, and a deficit does not become a positive recommendation.

**0:45–1:10 — Actual versus potential.** Reset the example. Show a £300 laptop-repair goal with £50 actually saved and a proposed £120 contribution. An illustrative, selected £20 spending reduction lifts the potential balance from £170 to £190. Actual savings remain £50; no money is transferred.

**1:10–1:30 — Optional education.** For hypothetical end-of-month £25 contributions over ten years, £3,000 would be contributed. Illustrative values: £2,589.46 at -3%, £3,000 at 0%, £3,859.08 at +5% effective annual returns. These are not forecasts; fees, tax and inflation are excluded.

End: a transparent, flexible way for students to choose an affordable action without needing to give up every purchase they value.
